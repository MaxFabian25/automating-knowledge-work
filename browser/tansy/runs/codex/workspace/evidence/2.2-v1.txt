£52,400k is reduced by £750k to £51,650k; associated cost of sales falls by £340k. Operating profit falls by
£410k from £4,960k to £4,550k. The £103k tax credit reduces the retained earnings adjustment to £307k.

premature revenue and were deferred by the correction. They were released in FY2026, when the
management accounts also recorded those costs, so no additional FY2026 audit adjustment is needed for

expensed; the FY2026 revenue correction therefore has no cost effect.
