## Points I checked specifically

- **Versions.** With "Show superseded versions" on, the index has 32 entries. The only superseded document is 4.1 v1, replaced by 4.1 v2 on 19 Oct 2026. The answers rely on 4.1 v2, as Q-12 directs. Every other document cited is at v1, its only version.
- **Request 1 (LTM).** 2.3 v1 P&L H6:M6 plus 2.4 v1 P&L B6:G6 gives revenue 68,020. Rows 16 give EBITDA before exceptional items 9,260. Both are on the management basis, as asked. The audit adjustments in 2.6 don't apply.
- **Request 2.** Reported growth is 34,380 / 28,450 − 1 = 20.8%. 2.4 I6 agrees to 2.3 B6:G6. The organic definition is in 2.4 v1 Notes A8: Kilcarrow (acquired 1 Jul 2025, within 24 months of 30 Sep 2026) is excluded from both periods. That gives 30,360 / 26,570 − 1 = 14.3%.
- **Request 3.** On a comparable basis, FY2025 should be the restated comparative in 2.2 v1 (51,650 / 4,550), not the first-reported 2.1 v1 figures (52,400 / 4,960). The answers use the restated figures.
- **Request 4.** The 3.00:1 in 2.7 v1 is superseded by the executed amendment 2.11 v1: 3.25:1 for test dates 30 Sep 2026 to 31 Mar 2027 inclusive, confirmed by Q-07. The reported 2.84:1 is in 2.8 v1. I recomputed it as 26,640 / 9,380 = 2.84.
- **Request 5.** The answers correctly say the figures are not disclosed. The contingent amount is redacted in 2.2 v1 note 4. Q-09 is still "Awaiting documents", and the SPA is promised for a later phase. One minor gap, which doesn't change the verdict: the answer could also have cited 1.2 v1. It describes the consideration as "initial €9.6m plus an earn-out", so the room shows no separate deferred element. The agent's choice not to infer a zero deferred balance without the SPA is reasonable.
- **Request 6.** The 5% threshold on 62,090 is 3,104.5, which captures Wendmoor (9.6%), Larchfold (6.9%), Corrigal (5.2%) and Dunstreath (5.5%). Dunstreath is a former customer, so 3.1 doesn't list it; its revenue comes from Q-06 and 1.2. Thornacre is 4.8%, so it is correctly excluded. The list is complete: other continuing customers are under 2.0% (3.1 Notes A5) and other ceased customers are under £250k (Q-06). The combined share is 16,930 / 62,090 = 27.3%.
- **Request 7.** 2.7 v1, change-of-control section: the amendment (2.11) leaves it unchanged. It requires advance Majority Lender consent unless the buyer is a fund advised by Skerrow Capital Partners. Without consent, each lender may cancel its commitment and accelerate its share of the loans, by notice within 30 days after the change of control.
- **Request 8.** The total quantified claim is €642,500 (6.2 v1: damages 598,000 plus costs 44,500; interest not quantified). Schedule 6.1 shows damages only, and the answers correctly don't use it. At the closing rate of 0.8455 (2.2 v1 note 1, not the 0.8512 average), that is 543.2, so £543k. The provision is £210k (2.2 v1 note 5).
- **Request 9.** 4.1 v2 Census gives C9 609.8 (= 476.3 + 74.9 + 58.6) and Kilcarrow C8 58.6.
- **Request 10.** `cmp` shows the delivered 2.2 v1 PDF and 3.1 v1 XLSX are identical to the files served by the room. 4.2 v1 is marked view only, with no file in the index and no download control. I read it only in the viewer and did not download it. Not supplying it, and recording the restriction, is correct.
